Built for the grind

Know what's next.

Suggested

Every mode points at the weapon closest to its next camo, and each mastery camo shows exactly what still holds it shut, class by class.

Log it your way

Full tracking with weapon levels and kill counts, or Quick tick: tap the highest camo you have and everything before it ticks too.

The whole picture

Stats shows each mode's completion, its mastery ladder up to 100%+, badges, prestige camos and a class breakdown. Share a card when you hit a milestone.

The MW4 DMZ, mapped

The full Hajin Tac Map with fortresses, key rooms, exfils and caches, guide tables, and your stash read from a photo, all on your phone.

Always up to date

New weapons and camos arrive without an app update. Spot something wrong? Report it and the fix reaches everyone.

On your Home Screen

Widgets for the game and weapon you're working on, and camo app icons that unlock as you earn them.
