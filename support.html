Support

Questions, a bug, or data that looks wrong? Email support@camo-track.app and I'll get back to you.

Wrong weapon or camo data

In the app, open the weapon and use Report a data issue. Once it's fixed the correction reaches everyone without an app update.

Does Camo Track read my progress from the game?

No. No app can read camo progress from Call of Duty, so you enter it yourself: either full tracking with levels and kill counts, or Quick tick, where tapping the highest camo you have ticks everything before it.

Coming from an older camo tracker?

Export from the old app, then import the file in Camo Track and carry on where you left off. The older trackers live at camotracker.djr.li.

Premium

Premium is a yearly subscription or a one-off lifetime purchase, handled by Apple. To restore it on a new device, use Restore purchases on the Premium screen. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings; it renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
